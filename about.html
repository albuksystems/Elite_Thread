Our story

A modern fashion and lifestyle destination.

ELITE THREAD is a modern fashion and lifestyle destination offering carefully selected casual and official wear, footwear and everyday accessories, based on Biashara Street in Mlolongo, Kenya.

We put together pieces we'd actually want to wear ourselves — clothing and footwear that moves between an ordinary Tuesday and a day that calls for something sharper. The collection today is sourced from a range of suppliers, and it's growing with the same eye for detail it started with.

Our long-term ambition is for ELITE THREAD to grow into a recognisable fashion and lifestyle brand in its own right — eventually with its own designs, labels and pieces. For now, our focus is simple: a considered selection, honest descriptions, and a straightforward way to order.
